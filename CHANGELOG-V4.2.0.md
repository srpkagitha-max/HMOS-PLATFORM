# HMOS V4.2.0

- Moved Check Admission Approval from Institute Home to the bottom of the Online Admission form.
- Added Institute ID and Institute Password fields to Admission Fees Settings for automatic WhatsApp sharing.
- Added separate Online Admission and Manual Admission options.
- Manual Admission creates the resident directly without online UPI payment or admin approval.
- Manual admissions support bed allotment, fee plan, received amount and payment mode.
